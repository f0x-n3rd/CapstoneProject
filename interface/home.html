Clog Drainage

Good day. I would like to report a clogged drainage along Rizal Street, Barangay Poblacion, Odiongan. The drainage is blocked by leaves, plastic bottles, and other waste, causing water to accumulate whenever it rains. The standing water has become a concern for nearby residents and motorists, as it may lead to flooding if left unattended. I kindly request the concerned municipal office to inspect the area and clear the drainage as soon as possible. Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.
